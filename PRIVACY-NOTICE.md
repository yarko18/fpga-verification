# Privacy notice for license administration

Version 1.0 — 9 October 2026

This notice applies to license notices and contributor agreements administered
for the FPGA Verification project.

## Controller and contact

The controller is Yaroslav Mariukha, acting as a natural person and maintainer
of the FPGA Verification project.

Privacy requests may be sent to `yarko@proton.me`.

## Data collected

The project may collect:

- the natural person, legal entity, or organized project sending a Notice of
  Use;
- the Software version initially used;
- a general project category;
- a contact name or role-based contact address;
- email transmission metadata;
- contributor identity, account, authority, signature, and CLA records; and
- information voluntarily included in contribution correspondence.

Do not send confidential project details, customer names, product designs,
source code, test data, results, architecture, credentials, production data, or
other secrets in a Notice of Use.

## Purposes and legal bases

The data is used only to:

- record compliance with the applicable project license;
- communicate about notices, contributions, security issues, and license
  compliance;
- administer contributor agreements and granted rights; and
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where the GDPR applies, processing is based on performance and administration
of the applicable license or contributor agreement, compliance with legal
obligations, and the Licensor’s legitimate interests in administering and
protecting the project and its legal rights.

## Publication and recipients

Private Notices of Use are not published. Public pull requests, commits, issue
discussions, and contributor account information remain public through the
source-hosting service used to submit them.

Data may be processed by email, source-hosting, backup, and infrastructure
providers used for project administration, subject to their applicable terms
and privacy safeguards. It is not sold or used for advertising.

## Retention

Notice records are retained for the duration of the reported Project Use and
for the applicable legal limitation period afterward. No annual confirmation
is required. A person or entity may optionally report that Project Use has
ended so the retention period can be calculated.

Contributor identity, CLA, grant, and contribution records may be retained for
as long as the Contribution, related rights, or reasonably foreseeable legal
claims remain relevant. Records may be retained longer where required by law or
necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

## Rights

Subject to applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion,
restriction, portability, or object to processing by contacting
`yarko@proton.me`. Deletion may be refused or limited when records remain
necessary to administer a license or contributor agreement, preserve the chain
of rights, comply with law, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

A person may also complain to the competent data-protection supervisory
authority.

## Changes

Material changes to this notice will be published with an updated date or
version. A later privacy notice does not change the substantive license terms
applicable to an earlier Software release.
